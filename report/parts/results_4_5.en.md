Table 3 and Figure 4 extend evaluation from the 4K training length to 32K. Full attention is evaluated by direct, untruncated extrapolation. Sliding-window attention, the no-update control and TTT-E2E continue to process the 32K input under their own runtime rules, so the position-wise differences combine state compression, window truncation and extrapolation error. We do not label an inference-time 4K visibility mask as a matched full-attention control because it would alter the evaluation position distribution as well as the visible history.

The 32K experiment is a length-extrapolation diagnostic. It is not training at 32K and it does not replace RULER or LongBench task scores. Reading it with fast-weight norms, session-state measurements and position ranges helps distinguish state drift, local windows and distribution shift beyond the training length.
