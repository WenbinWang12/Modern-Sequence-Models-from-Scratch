The ablations change one declared variable at a time and use held-out PG-19 validation windows. Table 10 covers the presence and use of fast weights, inner-loop learning rate, update chunk, suffix length and sliding-window size. It reports held-out loss together with training-token counts and median step times, while the broader resource comparison is reported in Section 4.10. This keeps a slower configuration from appearing to provide a free gain.

The ablations explain the mechanism of the main experiment. They are not a license to select the most favourable configuration and rewrite the main table. Configurations, seeds, token budgets and evaluation scripts are recorded in run metadata, which should be checked before comparing loss differences across repeated runs.
