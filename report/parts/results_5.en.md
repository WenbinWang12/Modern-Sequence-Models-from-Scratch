**Do evaluation items appear in the training corpus?** FineWeb-Edu comes from public web pages, and public benchmark items also circulate on the web, so this has to be checked rather than assumed. Table 15 reports: no verbatim overlap for HellaSwag, PIQA, WinoGrande or OpenBookQA; under 1% of items for ARC-Easy, ARC-Challenge, SciQ, LAMBADA and MMLU, the largest being MMLU ({{ n(r.audits.benchmark_overlap.tasks.mmlu.items_with_exact_match) }} of {{ n(r.audits.benchmark_overlap.tasks.mmlu.items_probed) }}, {{ f2(100 * r.audits.benchmark_overlap.tasks.mmlu.items_with_exact_match / r.audits.benchmark_overlap.tasks.mmlu.items_probed) }}%). Even if every one of those items were answered correctly — or incorrectly — the task's accuracy would move by no more than that share, which changes none of the conclusions in Section 4.7. Of the {{ r.audits.benchmark_overlap.tasks.wikitext.items_probed }} WikiText-103 test articles, {{ r.audits.benchmark_overlap.tasks.wikitext.items_with_exact_match }} have a passage that occurs in the training corpus, which is expected: Wikipedia text is widely mirrored on the web.

The check has two known blind spots. First, it finds verbatim overlap only, not paraphrase, translation or answer-only overlap. Second, items whose normalised question text is shorter than 50 characters are not probed (which is why the "items probed" column is below the full item count), so PIQA and OpenBookQA are only partly covered.

**Duplication between training and held-out sets.** PG-19 uses the official splits. Comparing digests of normalised full texts, none of the 150 validation and test books is identical to any of the {{ n(r.audits.duplicates.pg19.train_books) }} training books, and no book shares a normalised "title + author" string with the training set in the PG-19 metadata. FineWeb-Edu's held-out set comes from a shard that training never used, but web corpora contain cross-shard duplicates: {{ n(r.audits.duplicates.fineweb_edu.heldout_exact_duplicates_of_train) }} of the {{ n(r.audits.duplicates.fineweb_edu.heldout_documents) }} documents first drawn ({{ f1(100 * r.audits.duplicates.fineweb_edu.heldout_exact_duplicates_of_train / r.audits.duplicates.fineweb_edu.heldout_documents) }}%) were identical to a training document. Those were removed, and every FineWeb-Edu number in this report is computed on the de-duplicated set. Near-duplicates, such as pages differing only in headers and footers, were not checked.

**What training actually read.** Replaying the sampler with the training seed gives the shares in Section 3.2: the two sources land within 0.2 percentage points of the 50/50 target. Training covered {{ f1(100 * r.audits.sampler_main.sources.fineweb_edu.corpus_fraction_seen) }}% of the FineWeb-Edu training shards and {{ f1(100 * r.audits.sampler_main.sources.pg19.corpus_fraction_seen) }}% of the PG-19 training set, and about nine tenths of the text that was read was read exactly once. Training is therefore close to single-pass, and the models had little opportunity to memorise training text by repetition.
